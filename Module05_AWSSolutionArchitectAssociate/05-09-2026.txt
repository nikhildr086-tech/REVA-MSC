Since the litigation history is a material aspect of the title and our proposed purchase, we request you to kindly provide the following details in writing for each of the cases referred to in the title documents:

a. Case Number
b. Name of the Parties
c. Nature and subject matter of the dispute
d. Survey Number(s)/property to which the dispute relates
e. Current status of the proceedings
f. Details of the final judgment/order, wherever disposed
g. Whether any appeal, revision, review, execution proceeding or other proceeding is pending or contemplated
h. Whether the proceedings have any direct or indirect bearing on Survey Nos. 12/1 to 12/11, the project land or Flat No. C-808
i. Confirmation that there is no pending litigation or court proceeding which adversely affects the title, ownership, development rights or the Promoter's right to sell and convey Flat No. C-808.
